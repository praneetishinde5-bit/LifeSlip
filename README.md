# LifeSlip — Your Life, In Receipts

LifeSlip turns your everyday digital data—like Spotify music history or household expense spreadsheets—into authentic-looking thermal paper receipts[cite: 2].

## ✨ Features
* **Easy Data Imports:** Drop in your own CSV files or use the built-in dataset presets[cite: 4, 5].
* **Dynamic Backgrounds:** The page colors shift automatically depending on what you're tracking.
* **Multilingual:** Fully supports English, Hindi, and Marathi.
* **Fun Extras:** Toggle a dark mode receipt, add a custom "VERIFIED" stamp, and hear a real printer sound effect when you download your image.
